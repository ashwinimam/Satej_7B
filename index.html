What is a Computer?

A computer is an electronic machine that helps us to work fast and easily. It can store data, calculate, and solve problems.

Main Parts of Computer

  • Monitor – Shows output
  • CPU – Brain of computer
  • Keyboard – Used for typing
  • Mouse – Used to click

Uses of Computer

Computers are used in schools, hospitals, banks, offices, and homes. Students use computers for learning, homework, and projects.

Hospital Medical Records
